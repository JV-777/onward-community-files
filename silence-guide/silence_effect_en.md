# Onward: Abilities Affected by Silence


## Effect Rule

> Prevent the ability on the hero's sheet, on all friendly spell cards attached to them. Also prevent any temporary, delayed, or triggered effects granted by friendly spells.

Errata:

- Temporary: “until end of this turn/round…”, “this turn/round…”.
- Triggered: “when…”.
- Delayed: “At the end of the round…”, “immediately after this round…”.



**[B1] [Silence, clarification](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3598967/silence-clarification).** BGG participant Mark Buxton explains the distinction between a spell resolving and the ongoing effects it grants ([reply dated October 26, 2025](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3598967/article/46791174)). For Siege Masters, he explicitly ties the outcome to whether Doomies have Silence when the delayed push would occur, rather than when the spell is cast ([reply dated October 27, 2025](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3598967/article/46798007)). In a later reply, he distinguishes immediate resolution from an effect that occurs afterward ([November 14, 2025](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3598967/article/46887639)). These replies are used as community clarifications, not presented as official rulings from the game's designer.

**[B2] [Silence on a reactive card and the stack](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3595496/article/46760949).** The reply clarifies that Silence does not prevent spells from being cast or resolving: immediate movement and a temporary armor increase are treated differently.

**Delaying the minion phase—unanswered.**
At the time of this report, no explicit answer has been provided. Therefore, only the stat bonuses on Bloodthirst, Biding Time, and Living Jungle are confidently identified as affected. This interaction will be clarified later.

## Abilities Affected by Silence

### Akimo

#### Kurumo's Brand — `twoTwoCard`

> Deal 3 DMG and BURN 1 ::target:: nearby enemy hero or outsider.
> The next time that enemy enters an area this turn, teleport Akimo there.

**Conclusion:** The trigger for the enemy's next entry into an area grants Akimo a delayed teleport. Silence on Akimo suppresses that teleport; the initial 3 DMG and BURN remain immediate effects.

#### Ishiro Unleashed — `threeOneCard`

> ~Additional cost: 1 HP.
> Akimo's next attack this turn has +5 strength and also deals BURN.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Akimo suppresses the enhancement to his next attack this turn: +5 strength and the additional BURN. This is a temporary effect, rather than an attack made as the card resolves.

#### Bloodthirst — `fourOneCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Akimo has +3 initiative and +1 strength this round.
> The next minion phase is delayed until the end of this round.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Akimo suppresses the +3 initiative and +1 strength for the round. Its interaction with delaying the minion phase remains unanswered; see the note on minion-phase timing.

### Shyllavi

#### Wildcat Form — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Shyllavi until she is defeated.
> She has +1 armor and "Retaliate: DISARM".

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Shyllavi: Silence on her suppresses the +1 armor and Retaliate: DISARM.

#### Biding Time — `threeOneCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Heal 3 HP to Shyllavi.
> She has +3 initiative this round.
> The next minion phase is delayed until the end of this round.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Shyllavi suppresses the +3 initiative for the round, but not the immediate healing of 3 HP. Its interaction with delaying the minion phase remains unanswered; see the note on minion-phase timing.

#### Feline Pounce — `fourOneCard`

> Teleport to 1 ::target:: area exactly 2 areas away.
> If you do, SILENCE 1 hero there and prevent their next cast action this turn.

**Conclusion:** Conditional: the card allows a friendly hero to be chosen, since it says “1 hero” rather than “enemy hero.” For that recipient, preventing their next cast action this turn is a temporary effect granted by a friendly spell and is suppressed by their Silence (the card itself applies SILENCE). This reasoning does not apply to an enemy recipient. The teleport and the application of the status are not delayed effects.

### Nantaca

#### Living Jungle — `fourOneCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase and only if Nantaca is in a jungle.
> Teleport to 1 ::target:: jungle area anywhere without enemy heroes.
> She has +2 initiative this round.
> The next minion phase is delayed until the end of this round.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Nantaca suppresses the +2 initiative for the round; the initial teleport remains an immediate effect. Its interaction with delaying the minion phase remains unanswered; see the note on minion-phase timing.

### Haburat

#### Overdue Execution — `threeOneCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Haburat has +X initiative and +X strength this round, where X is the Day number.
> Choose 1 enemy hero anywhere, Haburat can only attack them this round.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Haburat suppresses the temporary +X initiative, +X strength, and the restriction limiting his attacks to the chosen hero for the round. A friendly source does not mean that only the beneficial parts of the effect are suppressed.

#### Weave — `fourOneCard`

> Choose 1 ::target:: nearby enemy.
> Haburat's next attack this turn ignores the "Weak Enemies" rule and deals any excess DMG to the chosen enemy.
> Excess DMG is any DMG beyond what is needed to defeat the target.

**Conclusion:** The modification to Haburat's next attack this turn is a temporary effect: Silence on Haburat suppresses both ignoring the Weak Enemies rule and dealing excess damage to the chosen enemy.

#### Lightning Blades — `ultimate`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Double the AP on Haburat's plan card this round.

**Conclusion:** Doubling the AP on the plan card applies “this round”: I interpret this as a temporary modifier to Haburat that is suppressed by Silence on him, rather than an immediate gain of AP.

### Sakoshi

#### Shuriken Storm — `ultimate`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Sakoshi gains 3 AP.
> This round, he can take attack actions, but may only target heroes and/or outsiders.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Sakoshi suppresses the permission to take attack actions granted for the round, along with its targeting restriction. The 3 AP gained immediately are not retroactively removed.

### Vorhild

#### Ermine Form — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Vorhild until she declares an attack or cast action.
> She has Tough during any hero's turn.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Vorhild. Silence on her suppresses the Tough it grants; the condition governing how long the card stays attached does not make the effect immediate.

### Khepiax

#### Chain Lightning — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Additional cost: discard 1 card from hand.
> Khepiax's next attack this turn may target up to 3 additional enemies in her area.

**Conclusion:** The additional targets for Khepiax's next attack this turn are a temporary effect, suppressed by Silence on her.

### Setheru

#### Word of Command — `fourOneCard`

> ~Channel only during your shop phase.
> For every 2 charges on this, choose one, same or different effect:
> - Draw 1 card.
> - Displace 1 ::target:: nearby hero.
> - Setheru has +1 momentum this round.

**Conclusion:** Only the “Setheru has +1 momentum this round” option is a temporary effect and is suppressed by Silence on Setheru. Drawing a card and displacing a hero resolve immediately; the requirement to channel during the shop phase is not, by itself, a reason to mark the card as affected.

### Doomies

#### Siege Masters — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Immediately after this round, push with the friendly minions in Doomies' area.

**Conclusion:** The push after the round is a delayed effect associated with Doomies. It is suppressed if Doomies have Silence when it would take effect. This specific case is discussed directly by BGG participants [B1].

#### Join Us — `threeOneCard`

> ~Attach to Doomies until they are defeated.
> When a nearby enemy minion is defeated, heal 2 HP to Doomies.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Doomies and grants a healing trigger when a nearby enemy minion is defeated. Silence on Doomies suppresses that trigger.

#### BOOM!!! — `ultimate`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Immediately after this round, deal 4 DMG to 1 enemy nexus or tower within 3 areas ignoring its Tough and Retaliate abilities.

**Conclusion:** The damage immediately after the round is a delayed effect of Doomies: Silence on them when it would take effect suppresses it. This applies the same principle discussed for Siege Masters [B1].

### Astryda

#### Freeze Push — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase and attach to Astryda until the end of this round.
> Prevent the push of all nearby minions.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Astryda. Silence on Astryda suppresses its prevention of nearby minions' pushes, even though the card is not attached to the minions themselves.

### Kohai

#### Grit and Grin — `threeOneCard`

> Heal 4 HP to Kohai.
> This turn, he has +1 armor and when an opponent chooses a target for an attack they must target Kohai, if possible.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Kohai suppresses the +1 armor for the turn and the temporary trigger requiring opponents to target Kohai with attacks. The healing of 4 HP takes place immediately.

### Ekhrit

#### Stasis Push — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase and attach to Ekhrit until the end of this round.
> This round, prevent the push of minions NOT nearby to Ekhrit.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Ekhrit. Silence on Ekhrit suppresses its prevention of pushes by minions that are not nearby to her.

#### Windborne — `threeOneCard`

> Move 1 ::target:: other friendly hero within 2 areas up to 2 times.
> They get +1 momentum this round.

**Conclusion:** Silence on the friendly hero receiving the effect suppresses the +1 momentum for the round. The two moves take place immediately; I do not consider Silence on Ekhrit alone sufficient to disable another hero's bonus.

### Kumaya

#### Shroud Smite — `zeroThreeCard`

> Kumaya has +2 armor this turn.
> Then, attack with +2 strength up to 1 ::target:: outsider or monster in her area.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Kumaya suppresses the +2 armor for the turn. The subsequent attack with +2 strength takes place as the card resolves and is not, in itself, a temporary or delayed effect.

#### Smokescreen — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Kumaya until she is defeated.
> Enemies outside of Kumaya's area cannot target units in her area or the area itself.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Kumaya: Silence on her suppresses the targeting restriction imposed on enemies outside her area.

#### Explosive Resolve — `threeOneCard`

> Kumaya has +2 armor this turn.
> Deal 2 DMG and BURN up to 2 ::target:: enemy heroes in her area.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Kumaya suppresses the +2 armor for the turn. The damage and BURN resolve immediately.

### Heraal

#### Ironwood Smite — `zeroThreeCard`

> Heraal has +2 armor this turn.
> Deal 3 DMG to up to 1 ::target:: nearby monster or outsider.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Heraal suppresses the +2 armor for the turn; the 3 DMG to a monster or outsider is the immediate part.

#### Elk Form — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Heraal until he is defeated.
> When Heraal enters an area, he may deal 2 DMG to 1 ::target:: monster or outsider there. (Once per Heraal's turn.)

**Conclusion:** The friendly attachment grants Heraal a damage trigger when he enters an area. Silence on Heraal suppresses it.

#### The Wilds Ride — `ultimate`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Immediately after Heraal passes this round, deal X DMG to 1 enemy in Heraal's area, where X are the MP he spent this turn. This DMG ignores the Tough ability.

**Conclusion:** The damage after Heraal passes this round occurs after the card has resolved. It is a delayed effect, suppressed by Silence on Heraal when it would take effect.

### Ixatosk

#### Split Form — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Ixatosk until he is defeated.
> When the jungling phase ends, you may deal 1 DMG to 1 ::target:: monster or outsider within 2 areas.

**Conclusion:** The friendly attachment grants a trigger when the jungling phase ends. Silence on Ixatosk suppresses the damage granted by the card.

#### Counterstrike — `threeOneCard`

> Ixatosk has +2 armor this turn.
> Deal 2 DMG to up to 1 ::target:: enemy hero in Ixatosk's area.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Ixatosk suppresses the +2 armor for the turn. The damage resolves immediately.

#### Tunnel Gank — `fourOneCard`

> Teleport 1 ::target:: nearby friendly hero to 1 ::target:: jungle area without enemy heroes.
> That hero has +1 strength this turn.

**Conclusion:** Silence on the friendly hero receiving the effect suppresses the +1 strength for the turn. The teleport takes place immediately.

#### Skytear Predator — `ultimate`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Ixatosk has +X strength and +X armor this round, where X are the MP on its plan.
> Ixatosk's attacks can only target the outsider this round, ignoring its Tough ability.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Ixatosk suppresses the temporary +X strength and +X armor, as well as the attack rule for the round: targeting only the outsider and ignoring its Tough.

### Shafathi

#### Shafathi Shadow — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Shafathi until he is defeated.
> When he defeats a monster, earn 3 gold.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Shafathi and grants a gold-gain trigger when he defeats a monster. Silence on Shafathi suppresses the trigger; gold already gained is not taken away.

#### Dematerialize — `threeOneCard`

> Shafathi has Tough this turn.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Shafathi suppresses Tough for the turn. The piercing DMG and SILENCE against the enemy resolve immediately.

### Yami

#### Ghost Walk — `threeOneCard`

> Yami gains 2 MP.
> This turn, she can take move actions, and she has Tough.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Yami suppresses Tough and the permission to take move actions granted for the turn. The immediate gain of 2 MP is not retroactively undone.

### Gulbjarn

#### Fearless Lead — `zeroThreeCard`

> ~Attach to Gulbjarn until the end of this round.
> Gulbjarn has +2 momentum.

**Conclusion:** A friendly attachment lasting until the end of the round: Silence on Gulbjarn suppresses the +2 momentum.

#### Bear Form — `twoTwoCard`

> ~Attach to Gulbjarn until he is defeated.
> Gulbjarn has +1 armor.
> Prevent the "move", "teleport", and "displace" effects of spells and abilities of ::target:: nearby enemy heroes.

**Conclusion:** A friendly attachment: Silence on Gulbjarn suppresses the +1 armor and the card's prevention of move, teleport, and displace effects in enemy spells and abilities.

#### Iron Grasp — `threeOneCard`

> Deal 3 DMG, DISARM, and pull 1 ::target:: nearby enemy hero.
> Neither Gulbjarn nor the target can declare cast actions this turn.

**Conclusion:** The restriction preventing Gulbjarn himself from declaring cast actions this turn is a temporary effect of his friendly spell, so I interpret it as being suppressed by Silence on Gulbjarn. This does not automatically remove the restriction on the enemy target. The damage, DISARM, and pull are immediate effects.

### Zacoal

#### Towering Lead — `zeroThreeCard`

> ~Attach to Zacoal until the end of this turn.
> Nearby friendly minions have +3 armor.

**Conclusion:** A friendly card is attached to Zacoal: Silence on Zacoal suppresses the +3 armor bonus it grants to nearby friendly minions.

#### \*Whheeeeee\* — `twoTwoCard`

> FREEZE and teleport 1 nearby hero to an area adjacent to them.
> They cannot declare cast actions this turn.

**Conclusion:** Conditional: a friendly hero can be chosen (“1 nearby hero”). Silence on that hero suppresses the restriction on declaring cast actions for the turn, which was granted by a friendly spell. FREEZE and the teleport are immediate effects; for an enemy target, the friendly-spell requirement is not met.

#### \*Thud\* \*Thud\* — `threeOneCard`

> This turn, when Zacoal teleports a unit to an area, he may deal 1 DMG and DISARM up to 2 ::target:: enemies there.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Zacoal suppresses the trigger granted for the turn that deals damage and DISARM after he teleports a unit.

#### \*Clonk\* — `fourOneCard`

> Zacoal has +2 armor this turn.
> Deal 3 DMG to up to 1 ::target:: nearby enemy hero.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Zacoal suppresses the +2 armor for the turn. The 3 DMG resolve immediately.

### Khenui

#### Deceptive Lead — `zeroThreeCard`

> ~Attach to Khenui until the end of this round.
> Prevent the Retaliate ability of nearby enemies.

**Conclusion:** A friendly attachment: Silence on Khenui suppresses the card's prevention of nearby enemies' Retaliate abilities.

#### Elusive Spin — `threeOneCard`

> Khenui has +3 armor this turn.
> SILENCE or STUN up to 1 ::target:: nearby enemy hero.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Khenui suppresses the +3 armor for the turn. Applying SILENCE or STUN to the enemy takes place immediately.

#### Magnetic Presence — `fourOneCard`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> ~Attach to Khenui until the end of the round.
> Enemy heroes within 3 areas cannot attack unless they are nearby to Khenui or have 0 MP left.

#### Echo Dance — `ultimate`

> ~Cast only during your shop phase.
> Khenui has +2 initiative this round and may teleport to 1 ::target:: other area anywhere.
> If you do, deal 2 DMG and DISARM 1 enemy hero there.

**Conclusion:** Silence on Khenui suppresses the +2 initiative for the round. The teleport and subsequent DMG/DISARM are part of the card's immediate resolution.

### Tlakali

#### Ritual Heal — `zeroThreeCard`

> Heal 3 HP to 1 ::target:: nearby unit and remove 1 of their statuses.
> If Tlakali is in a contested area, the unit also has +1 armor this turn.

**Conclusion:** If Tlakali is in a contested area and the recipient is a friendly hero, Silence on the recipient suppresses the +1 armor for the turn. Healing and status removal take place immediately; if Silence itself is removed, the armor must be evaluated after that removal.

### Akhuti

#### Radiant Healing — `zeroThreeCard`

> Heal 2 HP to up to 2 ::target:: nearby units and remove 1 of their statuses.
> Any of those units not at their starting HP level has +1 armor this turn.

**Conclusion:** Silence on a friendly hero receiving the effect suppresses the +1 armor for the turn if the HP condition is met. Healing and status removal are immediate effects; if Silence is removed, it no longer prevents the bonus.

#### Silence Ray — `threeOneCard`

> FREEZE and BURN 1 hero within 2 areas.
> They cannot declare cast actions this turn.

**Conclusion:** Conditional: a friendly target is allowed (“1 hero”). Silence on that hero suppresses the restriction on cast actions for the turn as a temporary effect granted by a friendly spell, even though the effect is harmful. FREEZE and BURN resolve immediately; this reasoning does not apply to an enemy.

#### Peacekeeping Ray — `fourOneCard`

> You may remove 1 status from 1 ::target:: unit within 2 areas.
> Prevent the next time they would lose HP this turn.

**Conclusion:** When a friendly hero is chosen, Silence on the recipient suppresses the protection against their next loss of HP this turn. Removing a status is the immediate part; Silence itself can be removed before the protection applies.
